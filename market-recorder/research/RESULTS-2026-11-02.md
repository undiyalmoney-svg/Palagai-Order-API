# Breakout research, first pass (2026-11-02)

Status: PIPELINE VALIDATED, NO EDGE ESTABLISHED. Options and futures order-book data have not been collected yet
(the recorder starts on the next trading session), so Models C and D cannot be run and nothing below is a claim about them.

## Confirmed (measured, reproducible with the scripts in this folder)

1. Charge wall. Zerodha's published schedule (checked 2026-11-02) gives, for ONE lot of futures:
   Nifty (65 x 23,400) round trip charges about Rs907 = 14.0 index points; Bank Nifty (30 x 55,000) about Rs980 = 32.7 index points.
   A 10-point Nifty target earns Rs650 gross, so a winning trade loses money after charges. A 17-point Bank Nifty target earns Rs510.
   Rs350-700 net a day on one futures lot needs roughly 19-24 Nifty points or 44-56 Bank Nifty points of gross profit every day.
2. Every breakout event (opening-15-minute and previous-day high/low, 2026-04-01 to 2026-11-02: 504 Nifty events on 145 days,
   496 Bank Nifty events on 142 days), taken with no model, futures, 1 lot, 1 pt (Nifty) / 1.5 pt (Bank) slippage:
   net Rs -790 to -1,090 per trade for every target/stop pair tried (`rr_sweep.py`). Gross points are about zero.
3. Candle-only Model A, chronological 60/15/25 split by day with a one-day embargo, 130 Nifty and 118 Bank Nifty test events:
   Nifty AUC 0.58 / 0.63 / 0.63 (logit / GBM / RF) with 95% day-bootstrap intervals about 0.47-0.73; Bank Nifty AUC 0.48-0.50.
   Selected top 10/20/30% of Nifty events still lost Rs675-865 per trade after costs; the model-selected daily series had no green day
   because even a target hit nets about -Rs320 on Nifty futures.
4. Futures volume/OI/basis (Model B) on the only window with usable futures history (46 Nifty and 44 Bank Nifty sessions, 51 and 36 test events):
   AUC intervals are about 0.3-0.8 and A and B are indistinguishable. This sample cannot detect an effect. It proves the pipeline runs, nothing more.
5. Sample needed. To distinguish AUC 0.55 from 0.5 with 80% power you need about 820 balanced events per index, about 240 sessions at this
   event rate. Thirty recorded sessions give about 100 events: enough to validate data quality, not to find an edge.

## Not confirmed (hypotheses, untested)

- That futures volume, OI, basis, book imbalance or option-chain flow separate real breakouts from traps.
- That a Nifty AUC near 0.6 in 2026 is real. The earlier three-year study found 0.49-0.53; 130 test events and six model/stop comparisons
  cannot separate this from chance.
- Any option-buying P&L. Charges are far smaller (about Rs60-90 a lot) but spread, theta and delta are unmeasured until live option quotes exist.

## Data caveats

- Futures history exists only for contracts listed now: NOV FUT from 2026-08-26, so before 2026-10-28 it is a far-month contract with thin volume.
- Lot sizes are Nifty 65 and Bank Nifty 30 (current Kite dump; valid for the 2026 window analysed). Do not run this on 2025 or earlier without a lot-size table.
- Slippage defaults are 1.0 / 1.5 points per round trip; sweep before trusting any positive result.
